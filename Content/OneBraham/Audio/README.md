# Content/OneBraham/Audio

Generated by `Tools/Python/onebraham/build_audio.py`:

* `Mix/` - 12 sound classes, 3 submixes, 4 mixes (master, voice-duck, flashbang
  muffle, downed muffle)
* `Banks/DA_AudioBank` - every cue the code plays, with fallbacks
* `Attenuation/` - 8 attenuation objects from a pistol shot to a tannoy
* `Ambience/`, `Music/` - 11 ambience beds and 8 music beds
* `Reverb/` - a reverb profile per area, from the lobby's 2.6 s to the Skydeck's 0.4 s

Replacement recordings go in at the paths the bank names. The full cue list, the
mix targets and the recording brief for every missing wave are in
`Docs/12_Audio.md`.
